## The project in one sentence

RouteLogic Velocity simplifies compliance checks and shift handoff for frontline logistics coordinators, with the goal of increasing workflow completion without increasing compliance errors.

## Key evidence

- 69% of coordinators do not complete the workflow after route assignment.
- Compliance checks take 14.6 minutes compared with a 3-minute benchmark.
- Manual workarounds waste approximately 31 minutes per day.
- Coordinator NPS dropped from +18 to -12.
- Four out of five accounts cite complexity as a reason for churn.

## Selected feature

**One-Click Compliance Checklist** — a pre-filled checklist that helps frontline coordinators complete compliance checks faster.

## Research evolution

The research started with delivery drivers and the workarounds they use when RouteLogic is slow, offline, or crashes. After reviewing the Module 3 data, the project shifted toward frontline logistics coordinators because the quantitative evidence showed a larger and more measurable workflow opportunity.

Module 2 remains the original research record, while Modules 3–6 document the data-backed pivot and the resulting product strategy.
