# Problem Hook & Value Proposition — RouteLogic Velocity

> Module 1 · Develop Strategic Product Thinking — ★ Deliverable 1

## 1. Chosen scenario

**Path:** RouteLogic Velocity (B2B)

I chose RouteLogic because its B2B workflow and operational challenges are close to my product management experience in debt crowdfunding.

## 2. The strategic crisis

If RouteLogic does nothing for the next 12 months, it risks losing enterprise customers to simpler and faster competitors. This could increase churn, reduce revenue, and weaken its market position.

## 3. The moment of misery

Frontline coordinators struggle with slow and complex compliance and shift handoff workflows. Compliance checks take 14.6 minutes instead of the 3-minute benchmark, and coordinators lose around 31 minutes every day using manual workarounds.

## 4. Problem hook

RouteLogic risks losing enterprise customers because its slow and complex coordinator workflows waste time and force frontline teams to rely on manual workarounds.

## 5. Value proposition

For frontline logistics coordinators, Velocity will simplify compliance checks and shift handoff so they can complete their daily work faster and with less manual effort, because workflow complexity is reducing satisfaction and increasing the risk of customer churn.

## 6. Cold-read self-review

| Question | My answer |
|---|---|
| Is the business risk high-stakes enough to justify a new initiative? | Yes. Four out of five accounts mention complexity as a reason for leaving, and Coordinator NPS has dropped from +18 to -12. |
| Is the moment of misery systemic, or just an edge case? | It is systemic because 69% of coordinators do not complete the workflow after route assignment, and manual workarounds waste 31 minutes every day. |
| Does the value proposition actually remove the obstacle? | Yes. It focuses on simplifying compliance checks and shift handoff, which are the main problems shown in the data. |

## 7. Finalized hypothesis

Based on driver feedback showing that they use messages and phone calls to update dispatchers, and data showing that 69% of coordinators do not complete the workflow after route assignment, we believe that simplifying compliance checks and shift handoff for frontline logistics coordinators will reduce manual work and improve daily workflow completion. Success will be measured by a 25% increase in workflow completion after route assignment. Compliance errors must not increase, and we will make a go/no-go decision after 8 weeks.
