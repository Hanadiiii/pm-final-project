# GTM Strategy & Success Dashboard

> Module 6 · Launch Impactful GTM Plans — ★ Deliverable 6

## Go-to-market summary

RouteLogic will run a targeted engagement launch for the One-Click Compliance Checklist.

- **Goal:** Increase adoption and help existing coordinators complete the workflow after route assignment.
- **Audience:** Frontline logistics coordinators at existing enterprise accounts, especially those relying on slow manual workarounds.
- **Launch tier:** M — Targeted. The feature has meaningful retention impact but does not require a broad public campaign.
- **Channels:** In-app walkthrough, targeted customer email, and Customer Success outreach with live demos.
- **Enablement:** Sales, Customer Success, and Support will receive a product brief, demo steps, common objections, compliance safeguards, and an escalation process.
- **Timeline:** Eight-week controlled beta, targeted launch in Week 9, and post-launch adoption monitoring during Weeks 10–13.

## Success dashboard

| KPI | Baseline | Target | Measurement window |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Coordinator workflow completion after route assignment | 31% | At least 39% | 8 weeks |
| Average compliance-check time | 14.6 minutes | Move toward 3 minutes | 8 weeks |
| Compliance error rate | Control rate | No statistically significant increase | 8 weeks |

## Bad signal to watch for

Coordinators see the launch messages, but workflow completion remains below 39%, checklist adoption stays low, or compliance errors increase. This would indicate product or onboarding friction rather than a channel problem.

## Post-launch decision

Double down if workflow completion reaches at least 39% and compliance errors remain stable. If the result is positive but below the target, iterate on onboarding and messaging before expanding the rollout.
