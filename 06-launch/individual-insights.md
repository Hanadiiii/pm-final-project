# Individual Insights — Development Process

> Module 6 · Launch Impactful GTM Plans — ★ Deliverable 6 (reflection)

## Friction points

The hardest part was changing the project focus from delivery drivers to frontline logistics coordinators. The qualitative research showed real driver workarounds, but the quantitative data revealed a larger and more measurable coordinator problem. I had to preserve the original research while allowing stronger evidence to change the product direction.

Another challenge was balancing speed with compliance. The checklist needed to remove unnecessary effort without increasing compliance errors.

## Key learnings

I learned that product decisions should connect across the full lifecycle. Research defines the problem, analytics tests its size, the roadmap focuses the solution, experimentation reduces uncertainty, and the GTM plan drives adoption.

I also learned that a strong primary metric needs a clear baseline, target, measurement window, and guardrail. A positive result is not enough if it creates risk somewhere else.

## Aha! moment

My main aha moment was realizing that the best opportunity was not adding more features for drivers. It was simplifying one high-friction coordinator workflow supported by stronger data. A focused checklist could create more value than a larger collection of disconnected features.

## What I would do next

I would run the fixed eight-week A/B test, monitor workflow completion and compliance errors, and collect coordinator feedback. If the checklist reaches the 39% completion target without increasing errors, I would expand the rollout and move to the Shift Handoff Wizard as the next roadmap priority.
