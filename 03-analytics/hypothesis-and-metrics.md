# Hypothesis & Success Metrics

> Module 3 · Use Analytics and Metrics for Data-Driven Decisions — ★ Deliverable 3

## Finalized product hypothesis

> Based on driver feedback showing that they use messages and phone calls to update dispatchers, and data showing that 69% of coordinators do not complete the workflow after route assignment, we believe that simplifying compliance checks and shift handoff for frontline logistics coordinators will reduce manual work and improve daily workflow completion. Success will be measured by a 25% increase in workflow completion after route assignment. Compliance errors must not increase, and we will make a go/no-go decision after 8 weeks.

## Success metrics

| Metric | Type | Target | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coordinator workflow completion after route assignment | North-star | Increase by 25% | Shows that more coordinators can complete their daily work inside RouteLogic |
| Average compliance-check time | Leading indicator | Reduce from 14.6 minutes toward the 3-minute benchmark | Shows whether the main workflow problem is improving |
| Compliance error rate | Guardrail | Must not increase | Protects accuracy while making the workflow faster |
